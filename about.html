Who I am

Hey, I'm Nelfar Zulkifli

I'm a property and strata management professional based in Johor Bahru, currently Business Development Manager at SA Property Management Sdn Bhd. My work sits at the point where a development changes hands — assessing it at tender stage, leading the takeover from the developer, and setting up the office, procedures and team that will run it.

Before that I spent eight years managing buildings directly, handling the technical and non-technical side of running residential and mixed developments and working closely with joint management bodies, management corporations, developers, residents and contractors. I hold a Master in Management from UNIRAZAK Kuala Lumpur.
