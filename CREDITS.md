1. **Development photographs** — pictures of the actual buildings named on the project

### Facts found during sourcing — to confirm before publishing

These came from developer and listing sources while identifying the buildings. They are
**not** on the site yet, because they are not in the CV and have not been confirmed:

- **The Quartz, Melaka** — freehold serviced condominium, Jalan Siantan 2/6, Kampung Lapan,
  Melaka; single 36-storey block, 310 units, completed 2019; developer Modern Diversified Sdn Bhd.
- **Mosaic @ Southkey** — completed 2017; two 34-storey towers, 584 serviced-apartment units,
  five boutique pods, 41 shop-office units; developer SouthKey City Sdn Bhd.
- **SKS Habitat @ Larkin** — completed 2021; two 35-storey blocks on 4.52 acres above a
  five-level retail podium (Larkin Junction); developer SKS Focus Sdn Bhd.
- **Greenfield Regency** — Malay name Pangsapuri Dedaun Hijau; 561 units in five towers,
  completed 2014.
- **Encorp Marina** — two towers of 30 and 35 storeys, ~538–571 residential units;
  developer Encorp Berhad.

### One correction already applied

**Residensi Neksus Taman Pertama is in Kuala Lumpur, not Kajang.** The site previously tagged
it "Kajang" — most likely because MKH Berhad is a Kajang-based developer. The development
itself is a transit-oriented project on Jalan Cheras (postcode 56100, KL), beside an MRT
station. The tag now reads "Kuala Lumpur". **Worth confirming with Nelfar.**

### Deliberately NOT used

The audit flagged a substantial part of the archive as unpublishable, and none of it appears
here: a screenshot containing a live Zoom meeting ID and passcode; slides marked
STRICTLY PRIVATE & CONFIDENTIAL; another party's utility-arrears figures; live QR codes; a
CCTV console; developer CGI renders belonging to a third party; and any frame carrying the
Marki attendance watermark, which burns in street addresses and staff names.

Roughly half the archive contains identifiable faces with no record of consent. Those images
are not used. `Roof top.jpeg` was considered for a project page and rejected on that basis.
