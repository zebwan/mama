Encorp Marina

Stabilising operations at a waterfront development on the marina at Puteri Harbour.

Development

Encorp Marina — waterfront serviced residences at Puteri Harbour, Iskandar Puteri

Role

Business Development Manager, SA Property Management Sdn Bhd

Period

2021 – Present

Encorp Marina sits on the water at Puteri Harbour. Waterfront buildings are harder to keep in condition than inland ones — the exposure is constant, and the things it affects are the things residents notice last and complain about most.

The work on site

Stabilising site operations and supporting the on-site management team: establishing what needed attention, coordinating the follow-up, and keeping the reporting and communication steady while it was dealt with.

Walking the building

Most of what actually needs doing is found by walking the site rather than reading a report about it. A door that no longer closes properly, a certificate about to lapse, a pump that sounds wrong — none of those arrive in an inbox.
