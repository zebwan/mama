# CONTENT_MAPPING.md

**Phase 5 deliverable — section-by-section content decisions.**

Reference template: `https://danielcross.framer.website/`
Content source: `RESUME_ANALYSIS.md` (derived from the three-page CV)

Confirmed with the client before writing this document:
- **Contact:** form + one email address only. **No phone number.**
- **Images:** her CV headshot + only the 16 photographs cleared in the Phase 1 asset audit
  (no identifiable faces, no GPS watermarks, no confidential text).
- **Scope:** four pages — Home, About, Projects, Contact — plus two case-study pages for the only
  two pieces of work with genuine documented outcomes.

---

## Standing rules applied throughout

1. **No prices.** She is an employee, not a freelancer. Every price pill in the template is removed.
2. **No invented numbers.** The only quantified fact is RM1.3 million in TNB credit notes. Counts of
   developments come from counting the CV's own lists.
3. **No testimonials, no client logos, no awards, no ratings, no follower counts.**
4. **No "available for work".** Nothing indicates she is job-seeking.
5. **Photographs are representative, not documentary.** Except where noted, a photo illustrates the
   *type* of work described, not the specific named development. No caption or alt text claims a
   photo was taken at a particular site. This is stated here because the template's project cards
   overlay a project name on an image, which would otherwise imply the photo is of that site.
6. **Third-party personal data never appears.** The two referees named on the CV, and their mobile
   numbers, are absent from every page.
